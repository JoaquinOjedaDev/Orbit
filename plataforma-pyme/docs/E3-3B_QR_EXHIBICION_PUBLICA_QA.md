# E3-3B — Public display QR, non-binding

## Objective

For public academic evaluation (two users and multiple companies), show a **Synthetic QR not payable**, the cart breakdown and the amount. Without creating remote commands, without connecting seller/box, without supposed confirmed payments and without affecting sales, cash or stock. It does not replace the flow TEST Payment Market B6/B7 that has already been verified locally.

## Implementation

- In public render: `PYME_QR_EXHIBITION_ENABLED=1` and in the frontend build `VITE_PYME_QR_EXHIBITION=1`.
- Not to be enabled `PYME_DISPOSABLE_QA`, `PYME_MP_QR_SHARED_QA_ENABLED`, `PYME_MP_SHARED_QA_ORDERS_ENABLED`, `PYME_MP_QR_TEST_ENABLED`, `PYME_ENABLE_QR_DEMO_LAB` ni `PYME_ENABLE_QR_FINALIZATION_QA`: API rejects when booting any dangerous combination with display mode.
- The fictional reference QR generated by `render_demo_qr_data_uri`: contains `PYME-DEMO-QR:<referencia>` y **does not contain** Payment URL, alias, seller token or a static payment market QR.
- A DEMO Link is enabled **internal and fictitious** by company at the first attempt to exhibit, because the QR snapshot requires `merchant_connection_id` with FK. No remote call or Pay Market account connected.
- The public UI of the POS allows you to create the attempt without entering in to Means of collection, shows the total amount and allows you to read the detail. In Means of collection it is indicated that no account/box is linked.
- The attempt screen uses the fictitious QR, avoids consulting official integrations and does not display buttons to create, reconcile, cancel or set remote commands. The approval/conciliation laboratory remains off.
- El QR **is not a public link for buyers**. It is not used to pay or access without a session the private commercial detail. Details are shown only within the authenticated area of the seller.

## Scope of business

- A QR attempt of display and an initial event audited in PostgreSQL is created; **no** a sale, collection, cash or inventory outage is recorded.
- To check a real sale with stock, use the POS with a declared payment means and explicit confirmation, separated from the QR.
- Operations and history remain isolated by `business_id`, session and role. Users from different companies cannot consult the exhibitions of others.
- The idempotency of the request_id and recovery of pending attempts are maintained.

## Validation

- Build TypeScript and Vite Frontend, npm test:qr, Public Docker React+FastAPI and Pytest backend suite.
- Test two different users: company A displays QR display with cart A; company B with cart B. Verify that user B cannot consult the reference or attempt A. Confirm that there are no new commands `mp_shared_qa_orders`/`mp_test_orders`sales and movements `stock_movements` For exhibiting.
- The new `render.yaml` enable display, but **no** installs MP credentials on Render. `DB_HOST`, `DB_USER`, `POSTGRES_PASSWORD` They're private secrets loaded directly into Render.
- Supabase must keep Data API disabled; the tables of `public` have grats and RLS disabled. Do not enable it for demonstration.

## Reversal

Change `PYME_QR_EXHIBITION_ENABLED=0` and deploy frontend without `VITE_PYME_QR_EXHIBITION` to return to the previous mode. It does not require new migrations or affect B6/B7 data.
